Engineering approval

Every order is checked by an engineer

Instant prices make quoting fast. Engineering review makes sure the part works.

  • Laminate check. Classical laminate theory with ply-by-ply failure analysis against your stated loads.
  • Flat ply patterns. Cut patterns and drape prediction so the builder cuts each ply right the first time.
  • Build check. Ply drops, radii, inserts, tolerances and finish, confirmed with the builder before cutting.
  • Approved drawing package. Laminate schedule, drawings and cut files, signed off before the firm quote.
